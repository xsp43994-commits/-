# S09

## 中文

三个核心学习模型的训练回报动态。每个观测是16回合训练批次的平均episode return；淡线为独立种子，粗线和阴影为种子中位数与四分位距，未做时间平滑。

## English

Training-return dynamics for the three core learned models. Each observation is the mean episode return of one 16-episode training batch; pale lines are independent seeds and bold lines/bands are the seed median/IQR without temporal smoothing.
