Training-batch learning dynamics of the three principal learners. Each model has five independent training seeds. Pale lines denote individual seeds, thick lines denote the seed median at each actually recorded episode, and shaded bands denote the interquartile range. Each seed trajectory contains 192 formal training records, without regular-grid resampling, moving averages, or interpolation. The ordinate is training-rollout batch priority-weighted coverage; its variation reflects exploration, sampled training tasks, and random seeds and does not by itself establish generalization or final test performance.
