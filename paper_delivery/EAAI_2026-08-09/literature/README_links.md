# EAAI exemplars and verified reference links

## Twelve EAAI Original Research exemplars

1. [Deep reinforcement learning for periodic Unmanned Aerial Vehicle task allocation and path planning with a Fixed Nest Station in power inspection](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2026.115219) — 2026; Exact-neighbour UAV inspection allocation, endurance-aware pruning, and routing narrative.
2. [Asynchronous multithreading reinforcement learning with attention-based significance measurement for collision-free robot navigation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2026.113779) — 2026; Attention-assisted reinforcement-learning navigation and collision-free evaluation.
3. [Situation-aware deep reinforcement learning for unmanned aerial vehicle swarm navigation in dynamic multi-obstacle environments](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2025.113518) — 2026; UAV swarm navigation under dynamic obstacles and situation awareness.
4. [Evaluating reinforcement learning-based neural controllers for quadcopter navigation in windy conditions](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2025.112090) — 2025; Quadcopter navigation under wind perturbations and controller benchmarking.
5. [Human-in-the-loop reinforcement learning for dynamic soaring: A trajectory planning and control integrated system](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2025.111219) — 2025; Aircraft trajectory planning, control integration, and engineering validation storyline.
6. [Reinforcement learning based multi-perspective motion planning of manned electric vertical take-off and landing vehicle in urban environment with wind fields](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2025.110392) — 2025; Urban aerial motion planning with wind-field uncertainty.
7. [Path planning via reinforcement learning with closed-loop motion control and field tests](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2024.109870) — 2025; Closed-loop RL path planning with field-test evidence boundaries.
8. [Layered learning in a quadrotor drone: Simultaneous controlling and path planning using optimal fuzzy fractional order proportional integral derivative and proximal policy optimization](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2024.108926) — 2024; PPO-based quadrotor planning and control integration.
9. [Reinforcement learning based robot navigation using illegal actions for autonomous docking of surface vehicles in unknown environments](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2024.108506) — 2024; Illegal-action handling for autonomous robot navigation.
10. [A reliable traversability learning method based on human-demonstrated risk cost mapping for mobile robots over uneven terrain](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2024.109339) — 2024; Risk-aware traversability over elevation-defined uneven terrain.
11. [Subtask-masked curriculum learning for reinforcement learning with application to UAV maneuver decision-making](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2023.106703) — 2023; Curriculum design and UAV manoeuvre decision-making.
12. [Multi-UAV trajectory optimizer: A sustainable system for wireless data harvesting with deep reinforcement learning](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2023.105891) — 2023; Energy-aware multi-UAV trajectory optimisation.
