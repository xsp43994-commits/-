# English figure captions

**Fig01_method.** Fig. 1. Return-aware PPO–Pointer framework and evidence workflow. Candidate inspection points are encoded with priority-aware self-attention. The pointer policy scores variable-length candidates, while a deterministic feasibility mask excludes actions that cannot satisfy visit, dynamic, resource, and depot-return conditions. PPO updates use the frozen training protocol; all reported evidence is generated by the locked evaluation and map-level statistical pipeline.

**Fig02_coverage_priority.** Fig. 2. Coverage and priority effects on unseen synthetic maps and independent geographic digital surface model (DSM) simulations. (a) Map-level safe priority-weighted coverage across principal learning and optimisation comparators. Points are independent maps; box summaries are descriptive. (b) Priority-stratum coverage for PPO+Pointer and the no-priority-bias ablation. The priority bias did not produce a significant aggregate ablation effect, so stratum differences are reported descriptively.

**Fig03_safety_resources.** Fig. 3. Safety/return effects and resource use. (a) PPO+Pointer minus comparator percentage-point effects for safety and depot return under known shifts and hidden mismatch; intervals are the frozen map-level uncertainty estimates. (b) Median energy, distance, and time utilisation for safe routes. The return-aware mask maintained route feasibility, whereas the no-return-reserve ablation produced the dominant degradation.

**Fig04_time_tradeoff.** Fig. 4. Online planning time and quality–time trade-off. (a) Empirical cumulative distribution functions of online planning time. (b) Safe priority-weighted coverage versus 95th-percentile planning time; marker size encodes safe-route share. Classical optimisation can attain higher coverage in parts of the test set but requires substantially more online computation.

**Fig05_training.** Fig. 5. Training trajectories, stability, and sample efficiency. (a) Median safe priority-weighted coverage over five seeds with interquartile bands. (b) Frozen D6 training-stability and D7 sample-efficiency scores. PPO+Pointer and A2C+Pointer reach similar final coverage, while PPO+Pointer provides the stronger combined training profile.

**Fig06_transfer.** Fig. 6. Unseen-map performance and zero-shot geographic DSM simulation transfer. (a) Map-level estimates and uncertainty intervals on unseen procedural maps and eight independent Copernicus DSM regions. (b) Representative Taihang DSM simulation showing depot, inspection points, and planned routes over elevation. This is simulation-only geographic transfer, not flight validation.

**Fig07_robustness.** Fig. 7. Two-layer robustness. Cell values show retention relative to each model's reference performance for known domain shifts and hidden model/perception mismatch. Safe-route share is overlaid where available. The design separates perturbations represented during training from deliberately hidden evaluation errors.

**Fig08_ablation.** Fig. 8. Four-component ablation. Points show the Hodges–Lehmann estimate for PPO+Pointer minus each ablation, with frozen 95% hierarchical-bootstrap intervals. Removing the return reserve caused the only large, significant performance loss; the other individual removals did not support independent component claims under the confirmatory family.

**FigS01_performance_profile.** Fig. S1. Performance profiles based on regret from the safe priority-weighted coverage leader for each task.

**FigS03_oracle_compute.** Fig. S3. Oracle regret and online computation. Horizontal intervals show the frozen regret range; x positions use median planning time on a logarithmic scale.

**FigS04_scenario_heatmap.** Fig. S4. Scenario-stratified safe priority-weighted coverage across node count, terrain, wind, and resource-constraint factors.

**FigS05_failure_modes.** Fig. S5. Safety and return outcomes across robustness families and perturbation conditions.

**FigS06_all_training.** Fig. S6. Training trajectories for all seven learning models under the frozen 3,000-episode protocol.

**FigS07_dimensions.** Fig. S7. Seven-dimensional operational evidence profile. The 100-point aggregate is a post-hoc descriptive summary and is not a confirmatory champion metric.

**FigS08_sensitivity.** Fig. S8. Sensitivity of PPO+Pointer ranking to the operational floor and combined D6+D7 training weight.

**FigS09_synthetic_route.** Fig. S9. Representative fixed unseen synthetic task with roads, inspection priorities, and comparator routes.
