# Non-copy audit

## Scope

- The manuscript was drafted from the frozen v3.2.14 Fact Map, Claim Map, Evidence Matrix, and verified reference register.
- The twelve EAAI exemplars were used only for section function, evidence order, reporting density, figure narrative, and claim-boundary conventions.
- No exemplar sentence, result, parameter, limitation, or reference combination was inserted into the manuscript builder.

## Checks completed

- Manuscript sentences were independently generated from author evidence and then inspected for distinctive exemplar-title phrasing.
- No exact exemplar title was reused as a manuscript sentence or heading.
- Stable technical terms such as “proximal policy optimisation”, “pointer network”, “Hodges–Lehmann”, and “digital surface model” were retained because they are necessary scientific terminology.
- The near-neighbour EAAI paper is cited as prior work; its dynamic-pruning/topology-aware method and periodic scheduling claims were not transferred to the present method or results.

## Access limitation

Publisher/DOI links are supplied for all twelve exemplars. Several full texts are subscription-limited, and direct publisher PDF retrieval was blocked in the current environment. Therefore, the sentence-level comparison is complete only for legally accessible publisher text/snippets and metadata. Before submission, the authors should run an institution-accessible similarity report against all twelve full texts. This is an access limitation, not a scientific-data limitation, and it is not represented as a completed paywalled-full-text audit.
