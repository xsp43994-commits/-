# EAAI official rules snapshot v2

Verified on 2026-08-09 against the official Elsevier/ScienceDirect Guide for Authors:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/engineering-applications-of-artificial-intelligence/publish/guide-for-authors

## Submission identity

- Target: *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*.
- Article type: Original Research.
- Review: double anonymized; title page and anonymized manuscript are separate files.
- Word submissions must use editable, single-column source files.

## Desk-screening controls

- The abstract must explicitly distinguish the contribution to artificial intelligence from the engineering application.
- Undefined acronyms are forbidden in the title and abstract.
- The manuscript must be single-column.
- The work must present an engineering application of artificial intelligence and reproducible validation.

## Front matter

- Abstract: no more than 250 words; factual, self-contained and normally without references.
- Keywords: 1–6 in English.
- Highlights: separate editable file; 3–5 bullets; no more than 85 characters including spaces per bullet.
- Section numbering: 1, 1.1, 1.1.1, etc.; cross-references use section numbers.

## Required content and declarations

- Figures, tables and captions must be cited and supplied in editable or publication-quality forms.
- Research-data statement is required.
- Competing-interests statement is required even when no interests exist.
- Funding, authorship, acknowledgements and corresponding-author details belong in the title-page/submission metadata as applicable.
- Generative-AI or AI-assisted manuscript preparation must be disclosed before the references when used; authors retain responsibility for verification, originality and rights.
- References must be internally consistent; author–year citations and an alphabetical reference list are accepted at submission, and DOI links are recommended.

## V2 implementation decision

The anonymized manuscript will contain no author-identifying information. Unknown author-side metadata will remain as `[AUTHOR INPUT REQUIRED: ...]` only in the separate title-page package and Author Verification Queue. No graphical abstract is generated in this round because it is optional and the project prohibits generative imagery.
